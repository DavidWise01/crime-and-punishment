Aftermath and Witnesses

Those affected retain their own experience beyond an admission or a story's ending. An emotionally satisfying conclusion cannot establish that trust, safety or repair has occurred.

Four natural distinctions

01 · Ideas do not undo acts.

The novel contrasts Raskolnikov's abstract calculation with two deaths. A justification cannot overwrite the victims or the actor's responsibility.

02 · A victim is not an input variable.

Alyona and Lizaveta were people, not placeholders in a moral equation. The event must remain recorded as two losses, not one convenient plot step.

03 · The witness survives the explanation.

Someone else's neat conclusion cannot replace the experience or agency of those harmed. Source provenance remains attached to claims about what happened.

04 · Closure is not restoration.

Ending a film or completing a confession is not independent proof of healing. Repair requires later evidence and free participation by affected people.
