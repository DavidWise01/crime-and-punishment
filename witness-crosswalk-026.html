026 · Confession is not corroboration.

Two distinct source boundaries: Nikolai's surprise confession is an attributed statement, not demonstrated guilt. An accuser's change of opinion does not erase what was actually observed.

BOOK B026 · Nikolai

Part Four Chapter Six: Nikolai interrupts Porfiry and confesses to both murders; Porfiry doubts the account. Later the former accuser, a tradesman from the gateway, explains the limited observation behind his accusation and seeks forgiveness.
