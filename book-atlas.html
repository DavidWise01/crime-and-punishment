Fyodor Dostoevsky's Crime and Punishment, mapped from Part One to the final epilogue: 39 numbered chapters and two epilogue sections. Each entry offers a concise original editorial witness question or framing, not reproduced prose. Part One is complete (B001–B007), and Part Two continues with playable B008–B014. All seven chapters of Part Two are now playable; the other 27 reading units remain mapped, not yet playable.
