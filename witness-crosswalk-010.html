Four bounded observations

01 · A symptom is evidence of a condition, not a unique explanation.

Visitors see fever and confusion. Those observations matter without establishing which memories, acts or anxieties cause every response.

02 · Care does not grant omniscience.

Razumikhin's loyalty and Nastasya's practical knowledge help Raskolnikov. Neither relationship grants a full, unfiltered view into his private experience.

03 · Status produces reach, not independent evidence.

A prestigious public speaker can cause a story to circulate. Repetition from one source does not increase the number of independent witnesses.

04 · Quiet testimony has its own provenance.

A less powerful witness may have direct knowledge even if fewer people listen. Pressure and the cost of contradiction remain part of the record.
