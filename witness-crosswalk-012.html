Four bounded distinctions

01 · Financial leverage is not moral ownership.

Economic resources can change options without removing Dunya's right to make a choice or refuse an expectation of gratitude.

02 · A theory does not reveal hidden motives.

Luzhin's words, Raskolnikov's accusations and Dunya's absent perspective remain separate sources. Confidence is not access to another person's mind.

03 · Contradiction calls for comparison.

Different statements do not authorize a convenient third story. Preserve their origins and test each disputed detail independently.

04 · Corroboration has a boundary.

A genuine third source can support what it captures; it does not verify everything outside its frame, time or access.
