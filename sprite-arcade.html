03 / a thematic bridge, not a duplicate story

The book & the movie

CENTRAL CHARACTER

Raskolnikov ≠ Roseanne Skolnik

The novel follows a former student's theory of exceptionality; the film follows a teenager amid severe family harm.

SOCIAL WORLD

St. Petersburg ≠ American suburbia

Economic precarity and institutional power take different forms.

WITNESSES

Sonya / Porfiry ≠ Vincent / Jimmy / Maggie

Roles are not exact one-to-one equivalents.

CORE TEST

Can thought excuse violence? ≠ Can suffering excuse retaliation?

Understanding a circumstance is not the same as morally justifying harm.

CONSEQUENCE

Conscience / confession ≠ Accountability / testimony

Both explore what responsibility changes and what it cannot erase.

STRUCTURE

Six parts + epilogue ≠ One 2000 feature film

Separate chronologies; no invented film chapters.
