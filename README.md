# CRIME AND PUNISHMENT · CRP · UD0

Fyodor Dostoevsky's 1866 novel, catalogued into **UD0** as a book-world — the autopsy of a
rational crime that the criminal's own conscience will not let him survive.

Standing template adapted for the novel: The Arc · The Book · The Ideas · **The Question**
(the philosophical deep-dive) · **The Verdict** (does the argument hold — honest) · The Message.
16 emergents by emergence-nature (flesh / the ideas / faith & redemption / the nerve & fever),
full `.dlw` each. Public domain; literary commentary under the DLW standard.
