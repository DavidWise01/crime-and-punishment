## CRP dual-world sprite chronicle — 2026-11-09 · V001

The long-standing **CRP / UD0** novel archive and all original `agents/*` and `crp.dlw/*` records are preserved. This is a separately authored, original Canvas2D sprite-art extension, not a replacement for the established book-world.

### Two grounded works; two separate timelines

**BOOK · Dostoevsky, 1866.** Novel in six parts and an epilogue: 7 + 7 + 6 + 6 + 5 + 8 + 2 = **41 reading sections**, counting both epilogue sections. Constance Garnett's public-domain English translation is accessible through Project Gutenberg ebook 2554. The first playable book episode is Part One Chapter One, with a garret, stairway and pawnbroker visit. A book-wide map is present in the arcade; **the 41 units are a reading map, not 41 finished playable games**.

**FILM · Crime + Punishment in Suburbia, 2000.** Directed by Rob Schmidt, written by Larry Gross, starring Monica Keena as Roseanne, Vincent Kartheiser as Vincent, Ellen Barkin as Maggie, Michael Ironside as Fred, James DeBello as Jimmy, and Jeffrey Wright as Chris. This is a separate copyrighted film adaptation, not the existing original `suburbia.html` social-correlation essay. The sprite scenes are newly drawn interpretive motifs, not copies of protected film images or dialogue. Film coverage is analytical rather than a replacement for viewing the film.
