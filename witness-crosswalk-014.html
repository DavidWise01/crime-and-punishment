Four witness distinctions

Compassion is observable, not absolution.

Raskolnikov's help after Marmeladov's accident does not remove the two murders already recorded in the novel.

A family relationship does not reveal hidden memory.

His mother and sister see his suffering but do not receive all the knowledge the reader possesses.

A nearby position is not a clear line of sight.

Witness access depends on the exact place, direction and moment, including possible obstructions.

Secondhand words are not firsthand observation.

Record the original speaker and the report's time. Unknowns survive until grounded evidence resolves them.
