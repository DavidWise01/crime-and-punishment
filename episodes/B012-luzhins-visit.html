Luzhin's Visit

Pyotr Petrovich Luzhin visits Raskolnikov's cramped room, hoping to make a favorable impression as Dunya's prospective husband. He describes fashionable principles of self-interest and financial prudence, but Rodion sees in his attitude a claim to power over Dunya. Their conversation becomes a quarrel about dependence, respect and the limits of wealth.
